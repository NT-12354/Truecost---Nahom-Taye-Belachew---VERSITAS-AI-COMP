What drives the price

Pipeline

  • Data: College Scorecard net price for five family-income brackets at ~ four-year schools.
  • Model: a small neural network (2 hidden layers) that takes family income plus school traits (sticker price, selectivity, size, share of low-income students, and more) and predicts log net price.
  • Honest testing: schools are split into train, calibration, and test sets, so the model is scored on schools it has never seen.
  • Error ranges: calibrated on held-out schools, then checked on separate test schools.
  • The network runs live in your browser. Nothing you type leaves your device.
Limitations. Net prices are averages for students who receive federal aid, not a quote for you. Income brackets are the Department of Education's, and the top one is "$110k+", so estimates above that are the least certain. Borrowing is simplified (4 equal years, 10-year repayment at an assumed 6.5%, no interest while in school). Earnings are for former federal-aid students 10 years after entry. This is a prototype for learning, not financial advice. Use each college's own net price calculator and talk to a counselor before deciding.
